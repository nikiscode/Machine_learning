##Evaluation Metrics
 Very important for exams
 For Classification:
Accuracy
Precision
Recall
F1 Score
 For Regression:
Mean Squared Error (MSE)
Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
R² Score

##Overfitting vs Underfitting
 Almost always asked
Overfitting → Model memorizes data (high accuracy, poor generalization)
Underfitting → Model too simple

##Include solution:
Cross-validation
Regularization
More data

Feature Selection
